Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

How fast is server setup after payment? +
Your server is provisioned and online within 60 seconds of payment confirmation. Our fully automated deployment system handles everything instantly — no waiting, no manual setup required.
Which datacenter locations are available? +
We have 7 global locations: India, Singapore, Dubai, Morocco, Europe (Germany), USA, and East Asia. You can choose the closest location to your player base for minimum latency.
Are the plans really affordable? +
Absolutely! Our Minecraft hosting starts at just ₹30/month and VPS from ₹60/month. We believe premium hosting shouldn't cost a fortune — enterprise quality at budget prices.
Do you support mods and plugins? +
Yes! We support all major server types including Paper, Spigot, Purpur, Forge, Fabric, BungeeCord, and Velocity. Install plugins and mods with one click through our panel.
Is DDoS protection included in all plans? +
Yes, enterprise-grade DDoS protection is included in every single plan at no additional cost. Higher-tier plans get enhanced multi-layer protection with faster mitigation response times.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the dashboard. Upgrades are instant with no downtime — your server continues running during the transition.
Is 24/7 support available? +
Yes! Our support team is available 24/7 via Discord and our ticketing system. AI support provides instant responses, with human escalation available for complex issues at any hour.
